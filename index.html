HOW IT WORKS

Built around a secret key.

HideBit derives a 256-bit key with PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256, then uses a deterministic PRNG sequence to distribute the encrypted payload across RGB LSBs.

01 Random salt + nonce02 AES-256-GCM payload03 RGB LSB + PRNG embedding
